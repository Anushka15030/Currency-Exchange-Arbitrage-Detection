THE IDEA

Small differences.
Meaningful loops.

Currency arbitrage happens when exchange rates don’t line up. By moving through a sequence of currencies and returning to the starting currency, a trader may end with more than they began with.

Exchange Lab transforms each rate r into a weight of −log(r). A profitable cycle becomes a negative-weight cycle, which Bellman–Ford can detect.
